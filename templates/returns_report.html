Appendix

Definitions & methodology.

For internal review by ownership and the executive team. Definitions follow Ember's standard underwriting framework.
LP IRR
The internal rate of return earned by Limited Partner equity, calculated on modeled cashflows from initial contribution through final distribution.
Equity Multiple (EM)
Total LP distributions divided by total LP contributions over the life of the project. A 2.0x EM means $2 returned for every $1 invested.
Preferred Return
Cumulative annual return paid to LP capital before any promote distribution to the General Partner, typically modeled at 8% per annum on unreturned equity.
Return of Capital
Distributions that repay the original LP contribution before any profit-sharing waterfall begins.
Excess Cashflow
Distributions remaining after the Preferred Return and Return of Capital are satisfied. Allocated per the operating agreement's promote tiers.
Promote
The General Partner's share of profits above the LP Preferred Return. Reflects the GP's incentive participation; not a fee.
Net Cashflow
LP-level cashflow per period (distributions less contributions). The basis for IRR calculation.
Cumulative Net Cashflow
Running sum of Net Cashflow. Inflection from negative to positive marks the project's modeled break-even.
Vintage
Year of LP capital first contribution.
