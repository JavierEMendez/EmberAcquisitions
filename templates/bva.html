Budget vs Actuals

Budget (your upload) vs Committed (open Sage POs) vs Actuals (Sage GL), matched by project & cost type. Generate the template, fill the Budget column, and upload it back.

⬇ Generate Template ⬇ Actualize Pro-Forma
Budget − Committed = budget not yet committed to POs (negative = over-committed).  ·  Committed − Actuals = committed cost not yet billed/posted (the open commitment).  ·  Committed = total Sage POs · Actuals = posted Sage GL. Split this way so a PO that later gets billed isn't subtracted twice.
