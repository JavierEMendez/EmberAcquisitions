Rent Notes & Comments

Mallard market rent is lower because of one loss leader. Mkt assumes $2,750 rent taken over all units; occ avg rent assumes actual rent which includes two residents at $2,950.
The $400 gain to lease reflects that premium for the two residents who rented at $2,950, before downward adj.
Raven market rents and current eff rents includes a loss leader with a market rent of $2,497 which impacts overall averages.
2026 Budget assumes ~$50 increase in monthly rents beginning in May 2026; so the rents will increase later this year.
Loss Leaders are not factored as a loss to lease since an adjustment is made to market rents.
